A gray slaad stands in a gloomy, foggy wetland on the edge of a cursed forest, among dead trees and remnants of a stone road. It is a massive humanoid-amphibian chaos creature with gray wet skin, gaunt muscular silhouette, broad frog-like jaws full of sharp teeth, blank shining eyes, pulsing chitinous nodules on chest/arms, a dark spot seeping from its chest, and gray mist around its clawed hands. On the Sword Coast, slaadi from the Outer Planes appear where spells cross; this gray slaad does not attack anyone specific, because everything near it begins to sicken.
